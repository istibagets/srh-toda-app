COMMUNITY STANDARDS

Built for Safe and Transparent Transit

Accredited Fleet

Every driver and tricycle unit is registered with an official 6-digit MTOP franchise number and verified association membership.

Regulated Fares

Distance-based tariff rates regulated by municipal authority. Statutory 20% discounts are strictly honored for Students, PWDs, and Senior Citizens.

Terminal Dispatch

Continuous operations stationed at the Santa Rosa Homes Main Gate Terminal Center, operating daily from 5:00 AM to 11:00 PM.
